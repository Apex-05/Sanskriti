What is Sanskriti?

A living digital archive of India's culture — created not by institutions, but by the people themselves.

Our Mission

India is not defined only by monuments or textbooks. Culture lives in everyday practices — a grandmother’s recipe, a temple festival, a folk song sung during harvest, or a craft passed across generations.

Sanskriti aims to preserve these living traditions by allowing people to document their own culture directly on a shared map of India.

What You Can Do

Explore

Click anywhere on the India map and discover stories, traditions, and cultural practices shared by people from that region.

Contribute

Upload photos and write about festivals, food, rituals, crafts, and local knowledge from your hometown.

Preserve

Help create a permanent community archive so future generations can understand the traditions that shaped us.
